These are web resources developed as part of my professional development leave in spring quarters from 2024 to 2026. This was an effort to apply an approach to making short video reviews from a calculus-based physics class (discussed in Dunleavy et al. 2022) to a non-mathematical general education astronomy class.

Video + Text reviews

The final version of each video + text review is presented with minimal-formatting to simplify transfer to LMS. For many, early drafts of each topic video are included, as well, since these frequently included material that is useful for inclusion in pre-class or in-class instruction. For each video, there is also a link to a materials page that includes code for graphics, datasets, or interactives created as part of making the videos.
