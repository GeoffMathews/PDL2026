Materials for using gravitational lenseing to measure masses

This page links to materials developed or refined as part of developing the review video + text for using gravitational lenseing to measure masses.

  • Simulation (developed using Google Gemini)
    1. Gravitational lensing simulation focusing on forward modeling of lensing, rather than only tracing the paths that reach us (prompts). This simulation focuses on visualizing the multiple paths followed by light from the original source, and the role that an intervening mass can play in shifting light that was originally on a path to the observer to a different path, while causing light that would have originally missed the observer to end up reaching them, but from directions that create the illusion of the distant light source being at different locations. This simulation includes multiple tools aimed at its use for creating visualizations (adjust scaling, displaying or hiding numbers used in calculations, etc.)
    2. Original gravitational lensing simulation (not used in final video) tracing the paths of photons that reach us (source). Initial visualization familiarizes the user with the interface, and how the lower part of the view represents what is observed from Earth. Adding a cluster introduced gravitational lensing by that cluster, with the visualization at the bottom showing the multiple images of the distant source (though simplified to not including distortion introduced, as well).
