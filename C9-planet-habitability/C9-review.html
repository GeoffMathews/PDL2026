Determining planet temperature

Read the following text and watch the video to review how to determine the average surface temperature of a planet.

There are three steps to figuring out the surface temperature of a planet.

  1. How far is it from the star? A further planet will have a cooler baseline temeprature
  2. What is the albedo of the planet (i.e., how much light does it reflect)? A planet that reflects more light will be cooler
  3. How strong is the planet's greenhouse effect? A planet with a stronger greenhouse effect will have a higher surface temperature
