Sonification Info

Baseline Temperature (Piano/Triangle Wave): Base pitch corresponding to the black dot.

Temp. due to reflection (Flute/Sine Wave): Pitch drops as albedo reflects energy away (blue diamond).

and including greenhouse (Violin/Sawtooth Wave): Pitch rises as greenhouse effect traps heat (red square).

Pitch Mapping: 285 K is Middle C. It scales linearly up 2 octaves to 1000 K, and down 2 octaves to 10 K.

Volume: The parameter you are currently sliding dictates which instrument is loudest. Changing Luminosity balances all three sound profiles equally.
