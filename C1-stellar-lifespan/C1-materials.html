Materials for Calculating Stellar Lifespans

This page links to materials developed or refined as part of developing the review video + text for Calculating Stellar Lifespans.

  • Stellar lifespan calculator simulation, source (was revised for use in making animation, but dropped from final video). Shows a model star next to a model of the sun, on a balance that shifts to help emphasize relative mass of the two. The luminosity is also adjustable, and is represented by changing the relative brightness of the model. A Play button simulates time passing (1 billion years per second), with a Fuel Percentage displayed above the model star and the sun to help compare the relative rate of fuel usage.
  • Star graphics were edited from NASA resource "The Lives of Stars" (original)
